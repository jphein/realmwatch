# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant v2.1][cc].

[cc]: https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/

In short:

- Be kind, be patient, assume good intent.
- Disagreement is fine; personal attacks aren't.
- Harassment of any kind is not welcome and will be addressed.

The maintainer reserves the right to remove comments, commits, code, edits,
or issues that don't align with this Code of Conduct, and to ban
contributors temporarily or permanently for behaviour deemed inappropriate,
threatening, offensive, or harmful.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour to the maintainer at the email in the repo's
git history. All reports will be reviewed and investigated promptly and
fairly. The maintainer is obligated to respect the privacy and security of
the reporter.

See the [full Contributor Covenant v2.1 text][cc-full] for the complete
version, including specific examples of expected and unacceptable behaviour
and the enforcement guidelines.

[cc-full]: https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/
